Ramadan and Eid books and activities for kids

Ramadan is the month children remember most, when the house smells of iftar and the evenings feel different. A few well-chosen books and activities help them understand why we fast and make the month their own.

Before Ramadan: build excitement

Put up a Ramadan countdown calendar a week or two before the month starts, and read a story about fasting together. Talk about what your child would like to do this Ramadan: a good deed every day, learning a new dua, or helping with iftar.

During Ramadan: small daily habits

Children don’t need to fast to take part. A good deeds tracker on the fridge, a daily dua card at suhoor or iftar, and a short story after Taraweeh turn the month into a routine they look forward to.
