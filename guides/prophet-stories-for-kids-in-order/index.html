Prophet stories for kids, in order: a reading guide

Reading the stories of the prophets in order helps children see one long story: Allah sending guidance to humanity, again and again, until the final Messenger ﷺ. Here’s a simple path through all 22 stories.

Why read them in order?

When children meet the prophets one by one, in sequence, they start to connect them: Ibrahim (AS) is the father of Ismail (AS) and Ishaq (AS); Yaqub (AS) is the father of Yusuf (AS); Musa (AS) and Harun (AS) are brothers. The order also mirrors how the Quran keeps returning to the same lessons: patience, trust in Allah and gratitude.

Bundle I: In the Beginning (5 books)

Adam and Eve, Cain and Abel, Noah’s Ark, Hud and the Terrible Storm, and Salih and the She-Camel. These stories introduce creation, right and wrong, and the first nations who were warned. A gentle start for ages 4 and up.

Bundle II: The House of Ibrahim (5 books)

Lot and the Nation of Sodom, Ismail’s Great Sacrifice, Yaqub and His Sons, Yusuf and His Brothers, and Yusuf and the King’s Dream. A family story across generations, full of faith, jealousy, forgiveness and dreams.

Bundle III: Trials & Triumph (6 books)

Ayub’s Patience, Shuaib and the People of Madyan, Moses and the Evil Pharaoh, Moses and the Miser Qaroon, Dawud and King Talut, and Sulaiman and the Hoopoe Bird. Stories of patience, honesty and courage in front of kings.

Bundle IV: Light upon Light (6 books)

Yunus in the Whale’s Belly, the Life of Zakariyah, Yahya and King Herod, Isa’s Miraculous Life, the Companions of the Cave, and the Life of Muhammad ﷺ. The series ends with the final Messenger.

How to read them with your child

  • One story a week. Read it a few times, then ask: “What did the prophet do when things were hard?”
  • Find the dua. Many stories include a prophet’s dua, like Yunus (AS) in the whale. Learn it together.
  • Act it out. Younger children love building an ark or a whale out of pillows.
  • Connect to the Quran. Older children can find the story’s surah and listen to the recitation.
