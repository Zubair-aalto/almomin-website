Questions parents ask

Do you have Islamic books about emotions?

Yes: “Why Are My Feelings So Big?”, “Why Are My Cheeks Red?” (anger), “My Emotions Connect Me to Allah” and “What I Say to Feel Okay”.

Which books are good for Ramadan and Eid?

“Fasting or Feasting?”, “Eid Catastrophe” and “Eid on a Giant Glider”. See our Ramadan and Eid guide.

Do you have books for older children?

The Manha Khan chapter books and “Did You Know? Incredible Muslim Inventions” suit ages 8–12.
