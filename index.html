          Questions

          Good to know

          Can I buy a single book?

          Yes, most titles are sold one by one. The Stories from the Holy Quran prophet series comes in bundles: 5 books for €10 or 6 books for €12.

          How do I pay?

          Online at checkout with card, MobilePay, Apple Pay or Google Pay. If you pick up your order in Espoo, you can also pay in cash. Just message us on WhatsApp.

          Where do you deliver, and what does it cost?

          All over Finland. Matkahuolto costs €6.90 and Posti €7.90 per order, or you can pick up your order in Espoo for free. You choose at checkout.

          Can I place a custom order?

          Yes. Need a particular title, several copies for a school or masjid, or a gift set? Send us the details with the contact form.
