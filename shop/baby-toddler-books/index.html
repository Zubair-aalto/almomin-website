Questions parents ask

Which Islamic book is best for a newborn?

A soft cloth book such as “I Say Bismillah” or “The World Around Me from the Quran”: high-contrast pictures, safe to chew and easy to wash.

Are there bilingual Arabic and English books?

Yes: “Allah Made the World Around Me” and “My Aqeedah” are bilingual board books in Arabic and English.
