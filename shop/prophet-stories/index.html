Questions parents ask

What age are the prophet story books for?

They are best for ages 4–10. Younger children enjoy them read aloud; children from about 7 can read them on their own.

In what order should we read the prophet stories?

Start with Bundle I (Adam, Cain and Abel, Nuh, Hud, Salih) and continue to Bundle IV, which ends with the life of Muhammad ﷺ. Our reading guide lists every book in order.

What language are the books in?

The books are in English, with Islamic names and phrases explained in the text.

How fast is delivery?

We send orders within 1–3 working days by Matkahuolto (€6.90) or Posti (€7.90), or you can collect for free in Espoo.
