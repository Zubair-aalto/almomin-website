Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to send your books and keep our accounts. We don’t sell your data, and this site has no advertising or tracking cookies.

1. Who is responsible

Decent Enterprises Oy (Almo’min), Y-tunnus 2800683-4, Kilonrinne 10 B, 02610 Espoo, Finland. Contact for privacy questions: almomin.eu@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • When you order: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, what you bought, the delivery method and the payment status. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see them.
  • When you contact us: the contact form opens WhatsApp or your email program. We receive only what you send us, such as your name, contact details and message.
  • In your browser: your basket is kept in your own browser’s storage so it isn’t lost when you reload the page. It is not sent to us until you check out.

3. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To process, deliver and support your order: performance of a contract.
  • To keep bookkeeping records required by Finnish law: legal obligation.
  • To answer your messages and custom-order requests: legitimate interest / steps at your request before a contract.

We send no marketing emails unless you ask us to.

4. Who we share it with

Only with the services needed to run the shop:

  • Stripe: payments and receipts.
  • Posti or Matkahuolto: your name, address and phone, to deliver your parcel.
  • Netlify: hosts this website.
  • Google (Gmail) and WhatsApp (Meta): if you contact us through them.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and some site code from the jsDelivr network, so your browser connects to them and they see your IP address.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU/EEA. In that case they use the safeguards required by EU law, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

5. How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept for as long as Finnish accounting law requires (currently 6 years after the end of the financial year). Messages are deleted when they are no longer needed to help you, normally within 2 years.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, or receive it in a portable format. Email us and we will reply within one month. Data we must keep for bookkeeping cannot be deleted before the legal period ends.

You can also complain to the Finnish data protection authority, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.
